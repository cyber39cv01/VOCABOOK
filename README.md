# VOCABOOK
A project to make a fan-made revival of the 2011 vocaloid community site known as "MIKUBOOK". In order to contribute to this project, please e-mail me at: cyberfromsplashyt@gmail.com, I will then send a form for you to fill to see if you meet the requirements needed. Together, we will all meet our next favorite song!
